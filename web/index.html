skinhash in the browser

Finds invisible variations of your own Minecraft skin whose texture link looks cool (c0ffee…, many leading zeros). Everything runs locally on your GPU. Nothing is uploaded.

WebGPU is not available in this browser. This tool needs WebGPU: recent desktop Chrome or Edge (113+), Chrome on Android (121+), Safari 26+ or Firefox 141+ (Windows). Support also depends on your OS and GPU driver; check chrome://gpu or try another browser.

    Notes

    • Everything runs locally in your browser; the skin never leaves your computer.
    • Only fully transparent pixels change (the end of the left sleeve overlay, plus the watermark in an unused corner). Nothing visible is touched.
    • Every hit is rebuilt on the CPU, hashed again, compared pixel by pixel with your original and re-encoded with pako before it is shown.
    • Upload the downloaded PNG to Minecraft as is. Any editing or re-saving changes the id. Mojang re-encodes uploads, but hidden pixels survive.
    • The watermark (sokripon skinhash + version byte) is included by default so skins made with this tool can be recognised.
