skinhash in the browser

Finds invisible variations of your own Minecraft skin whose Mojang texture link or NameMC page id looks cool, like c0ffee… or many leading zeros. Runs on your GPU. Nothing is uploaded.

WebGPU is not available in this browser. Use recent desktop Chrome or Edge (113+), Chrome on Android (121+), Safari 26+ or Firefox 141+ (Windows). It also depends on your OS and GPU driver; check chrome://gpu or try another browser.

2. What are you looking for?

NameMC runs first, then Mojang. The phases add up instead of multiplying, so this is much faster than searching both at once.

    Pattern syntax c0ffee anywhere, ^ start, $ end, ? any digit, ?d 0-9, ?l a-f, [0-3] [05af] classes, {n} repeat, * any gap (^cafe*babe$). Mojang: ^ is the start of the full 64-digit hash, so ^0{12} is 12 leading zeros; without ^ the pattern must be in the link id (after the leading zeros). NameMC: the 16 digits. # comments and comma lists work. A hit matches any line.

    Counts, shapes and records. Spaces and capitals are free. Mojang: counts and shapes look at the link id (leading zeros dropped).

    #0{24,}at least 24 zeros ({n} exactly, {n,} at least, {,n} at most, {n,m})
    #?l{,0} #?l{50,}letters a-f: none / at least 50 (#?d digits, #[0-3] any class)
    ##{,9}at most 9 different digits
    #(dead|beef|cafe){3,}words (1-8 digits) found at least 3 times together, overlaps count
    (?)\1{11}one digit 12 times in a row ((??)\1{6} a pair 7 times = a zigzag like 7c7c7c7c7c7c7c; (?{3})\1{4} a triple, period 1 to 8)
    +{12} -{12}staircase up / down of 12 digits (no wrap from f to 0)
    ~{25}a palindrome of at least 25 digits
    ^f{max} 0{max}the longest run of that digit: from the start / anywhere
    A & Ball parts must hold: ^dead & #0{10,}
    ~{max} +{max} #?l{min} ##{min}record lines: no end, every new best is listed as RECORD ({max} for counts, shapes and runs, {min} for counts and ##), at most one per line. Records do not count as hits, the search runs until you press Stop, the best is kept while this page is open.
